Interpretation
--------------------------------------------------------------

This is not a single periodic crystal. DNA is helical, so each
phosphate-bound seed grew a locally monoclinic COM patch. After
DNA is removed those patches still carry:
  * the 6.29 Å a-chain (phosphate-templated)
  * the ~10.1 Å c step (outward from the helix)
  * the 3.84 Å intra-cell Ca–Ca is omitted (min Ca–Ca is 6 Å)
A global P2_1/n operation will not map the whole model onto
itself. Locally (one strand, ~10 Å) the symmetry is whewellite.
Shared Ca sites (B=10 in the PDB) are where two patches agreed
on the same lattice point — the start of crystal continuity.
